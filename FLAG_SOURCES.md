# Australian flag artwork

Retrieved 6 October 2026. The app bundles these assets for offline use; it does not request artwork from a network service at runtime.

## Authoritative reference

The designs and territory coverage were checked against the Australian Government's [State and territory flags](https://www.pmc.gov.au/resources/australian-flags-booklet/part-3-other-official-flags-australia/state-and-territory-flags) and [Australian territories](https://www.infrastructure.gov.au/territories-regions/australian-territories) pages.

The catalogue includes six states, the Australian Capital Territory, the Northern Territory, Jervis Bay Territory, and seven external territories. Jervis Bay has no separate flag. The national flag is also used for Ashmore and Cartier Islands, Coral Sea Islands, Heard Island and McDonald Islands, and the Australian Antarctic Territory. The Antarctic entry is an Australian territorial claim; inclusion is for travel recording, not a statement about the positions of other Antarctic Treaty parties.

PM&C identifies Norfolk Island's flag as official and Christmas Island's and Cocos (Keeling) Islands' distinctive flags as community flags that have not been formally adopted. Christmas Island's community flag is included and described accordingly. Cocos (Keeling) Islands uses the Australian National Flag here: the former Commons community-flag SVG was [deleted following a copyright review](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Deletion_requests/File:Flag_of_the_Cocos_(Keeling)_Islands.svg), so no unverified substitute is bundled.

The Australian, NSW, Victoria and Queensland assets are 768-pixel PNG renders of the downloaded SVGs, made with WebKit. The other seven assets are Wikimedia's PNG renders of the linked SVGs, resized to 768 pixels wide. Artwork is unchanged. Their 2:1 aspect ratio and colours are retained. No flag artwork was generated with AI or redrawn. The CC BY-SA artwork and any derivative artwork remain under CC BY-SA 4.0; this does not change the licence of unrelated application code.

The app's About screen provides the NSW and Victoria author credits, source links and CC BY-SA 4.0 licence link. Preserve those credits when redistributing the app or these assets. Government flag designs may have usage conventions independent of the image files' copyright licences; the app uses them only to identify the places represented.
