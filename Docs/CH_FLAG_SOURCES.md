# Switzerland flag sources

The 26 cantons are listed in the order of Article 1 of the [Swiss Federal Constitution](https://www.parlament.ch/centers/documents/en/20240301_BV-en.pdf). Canton IDs use ISO 3166-2:CH. Switzerland uses one Cantons group in the catalog.

The bundled files are Wikimedia Commons PNG renders, resized to 768 pixels, of authentic cantonal SVG artwork. Their source proportions are preserved, including the tiny bounding-box differences in a few older vector extractions from the Swiss Army flag regulations. All 26 source pages identify the artwork as public domain under [PD-Coa-Switzerland](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Template:PD-Coa-Switzerland). Individual drawing credits are retained below and in Settings → Acknowledgements → Switzerland.

St. Gallen uses the 2011 governmental drawing and official green, Geneva uses the cantonal government vector drawing, and Valais uses the clean vector from the Swiss Army flag regulations rather than the older PDF extraction with black star outlines. These are factual travel identifiers, not an assertion of government affiliation.
