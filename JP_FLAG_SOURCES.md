# Japanese prefecture flag artwork

Retrieved 16 October 2026. The app bundles these 47 assets (`jp_flag1` … `jp_flag47`, JIS prefecture order) for offline use. It does not request artwork from a network service at runtime. Credits shown in the app come from `jpex/JapanFlagCredits.json`.

## Needs attention

- **Hiroshima (`jp_flag34`): licence currently unclear.** When the bundled revision was current, the file page licensed it under `{{self|author=Alex Tora|GFDL|cc-by-sa-3.0,2.5,2.0,1.0}}` ([page as of 5 Nov 2021](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?oldid=605627189)). On 4 October 2026 the licence section was removed and the author changed to "Unknown" ([edit](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?oldid=1282957262)), so the page now carries no licence tag. CC BY-SA licences are not revocable, so the app credits **Alex Tora and Mike Rohsopht under CC BY-SA 3.0**. Recheck the page before release. If it is retagged as public domain, the credit can follow it.
- **Kagawa (`jp_flag37`): CC BY-SA 3.0** (dual-licensed with GFDL, `{{self|cc-by-sa-3.0|GFDL}}`). Attribution and share-alike apply to this image, so keep the credit in the app.
- **Hyogo (`jp_flag28`): not matched.** No revision of any Commons Hyogo flag file reproduces the bundled image. Details are below the table. The JSON credits the closest Commons file and says the bundled drawing is unverified.
- **Non-copyright restrictions.** The Osaka and Kagawa file pages state that use requires prefectural approval ("If you will use this file, you must get … Prefecture approval"; Kagawa asks for a 県章使用届, Osaka for a 府章使用申請). All 47 pages also carry Commons' `{{Insignia}}` warning, except Ehime's. These are insignia-use conditions, not copyright licence terms, but they are flagged here for review.
- No NC or ND licences were found. The other 45 files are public domain on their Commons pages (`PD-Japan-organization`, `PD-Japan-exempt`, `PD-ineligible`, `PD-textlogo` or `PD-shape`).

## How matches were verified

- The bundled PNGs are Wikimedia thumbnails rendered at 512 px high. Every image is 512 px tall. Each width equals MediaWiki's height-constrained thumbnail width (`File::fitBoxWidth`) for the nominal SVG size of the matching revision: 768 for 3:2 files, 732 for 10:7, 717 for 7:5, 727 for Ishikawa 440×310, 724 for Mie 728×515, 770 for Ehime 400×266 and 769 for Nagasaki 800×533. Each file also carries the same `bKGD 00ff00ff00ff` PNG chunk as Wikimedia thumbnails.
- Every revision of each file (259 revisions) and 11 alternative or variant files were fetched through the Commons API. Their 960 px thumbnails were scaled to the bundled size over white and compared by full RMSE, 8×8-block RMSE and worst-block difference. Each match was then inspected visually as a reference, candidate and difference strip. Wikimedia no longer serves non-standard thumbnail widths, so a byte-identical comparison was not possible.
- In each case the matching revision was the one current on Commons between 12 and 25 January 2022. Saga's 12 January 2022 upload matches; Yamanashi's 25 January 2022 upload does not. This is consistent with a single download in mid-January 2022. Ten pages now show a different drawing from the bundled one, so the JSON names the revision date for those.
- **Exact** means the same revision, with differences limited to anti-aliased edges from rescaling. Block RMSE is ≤ 0.016, and no area differs by more than about one pixel of edge position. **Near-identical** means the same artwork, with a small difference explained in the table. **Not matched** means no Commons revision reproduces the image.
- Authors are as stated on the file page. Where the bundled revision is no longer current, or the author field was rewritten after January 2022, the credit follows the page as it stood when the bundled revision was current. The table notes the current wording.

## Unmatched: Hyogo

The bundled `28_flag_Hyogo.png` is 768×512 and opaque, in cyan #01A0C7 with four white waves and the 兵 mark. The following were checked:

- all five revisions of "Flag of Hyogo Prefecture.svg"
- its redirects, "Flag of Hyōgo Prefecture.svg" and "Flag of Hyogo.svg"
- "Flag of Hyogo 2.svg" and "Flag of Hyogo Prefecture.png"
- the file's upload log
- Wikidata's flag image (P41)
- local copies on English and Japanese Wikipedia

None is 3:2. The revisions since February 2022 share the bundled colour but have different wave proportions (RMSE ≥ 0.21). The revision current in January 2022 was a 300×210 drawing in #6499CD. The bundled Hyogo image therefore did not come from the same January 2022 download as the others, and its source is unknown.

**Recommended action:** replace `jp_flag28` with a PNG render of the current [Flag of Hyogo Prefecture.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Hyogo_Prefecture.svg), which is public domain. The existing credit (Guilherme Paula and Maanshen) would then be exact, and its note can change to the standard wording. Until then the JSON note states that the bundled drawing is unverified.

The Wikimedia renders are unchanged PNG conversions of the linked SVGs at 512 px high. No flag artwork was generated with AI or redrawn.
