# United Kingdom catalog and flag sources

Verified on 6 October 2026.

## Administrative scope

The catalog contains the four constituent countries: England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. The UK Government describes these four parts in its [annual report on the UK internal market](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/annual-report-on-the-operation-of-the-uk-internal-market-2023-to-2024/annual-report-on-the-operation-of-the-uk-internal-market-2023-to-2024). Permanent IDs use ISO 3166-2:GB: `GB-ENG`, `GB-SCT`, `GB-WLS` and `GB-NIR`.

This collection does not include Crown Dependencies or Overseas Territories as constituent countries. Those places have separate entries in the world catalog where applicable.

Northern Ireland uses the Union Flag. The Northern Ireland Office confirmed in its [19 October 2023 Parliamentary answer](https://questions-statements.parliament.uk/written-questions/detail/2023-10-13/201972) that it is the only official flag of Northern Ireland. The app does not substitute the historical Ulster Banner or an invented flag.

## Artwork

All four flags are 768-pixel PNG renders of unmodified SVG artwork from [hampusborgos/country-flags](https://github.com/hampusborgos/country-flags). The publisher identifies its Wikimedia-derived flag artwork as public domain in the [repository README](https://github.com/hampusborgos/country-flags#accurate-country-flags). Source files were taken from the already downloaded repository archive used for the world catalog.
