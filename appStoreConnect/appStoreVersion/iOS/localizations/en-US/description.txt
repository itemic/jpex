Prefectures started as a simple way to track which of Japan's 47 prefectures you've been to. Now it goes around the world.

Mark the countries you've been to, then go deeper: Japan's prefectures, America's states, Canada's provinces, France's departments, the UK's councils, and the states, provinces and regions of more than 130 countries.

HOW WELL YOU KNOW IT
Every place gets a level, from passing through to having lived there. Rename the levels, recolour them, or add your own.

YOUR MAP
Each list has a map that fills in as you go. Zoom in on any country and watch your travels light up.

QUIZ YOURSELF
Find places on the map, name them from their flag, capital, outline or code, drag shapes to where they belong, or list every one you can remember.

GROUPS OF COUNTRIES
See how far you've got through the EU, the G20, ASEAN, the Commonwealth and more, or make groups of your own.

WORLD TIME
Spin the World to wind every clock on Earth forward or back, and tap any country for its time.

MAKE IT YOURS
Over 70 app icons, from the classic Japan map in five colours to Chinese and European licence plates.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything stays on your device. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.
