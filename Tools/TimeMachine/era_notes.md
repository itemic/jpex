# Time Machine era notes

Research notes from the era fragments (`Tools/TimeMachine/eras/*.json`), collected when they were merged into `jpex/TimeMachineEras.json`.

## 1815: The World After Napoleon

- Era date taken as late 1815, after the Final Act of Vienna (June) and the Second Treaty of Paris (November).
- Colonies occupied by Britain in the war but restored by the 1814 treaties are shown with their restored owners: Java/Dutch East Indies (handed back Aug 1816), Surinam, Curaçao, Aruba, Sint Maarten (1816), Guadeloupe and Martinique (re-occupied by Britain 1815–16). French Guiana was still held by Portugal–Brazil until 1817 but is shown French. Saint-Louis and Gorée (Senegal) were British-held until 1817; SN is shown as Wolof/Futa Toro lands since the posts are tiny.
- DE~palatinate: mostly Prussian (Lower Rhine, Saar); the Bavarian Palatinate (assigned 1816), Rheinhessen (Hesse-Darmstadt) and Oldenburg's Birkenfeld are folded in.
- DE~badenwurttemberg: Württemberg and the Grand Duchy of Baden share one piece; given to Württemberg with a combined label.
- DE~lowersaxony: Hanover, with Oldenburg, Brunswick and Bremen folded in. DE~hesse: Hesse-Kassel, Hesse-Darmstadt, Nassau and Frankfurt grouped as 'Hessian states'. DE~thuringia: Saxon duchies, Schwarzburg, Reuss and Prussian Erfurt grouped. DE~mecklenburg includes Western Pomerania, which went to Prussia in 1815. DE~holstein includes the free cities of Hamburg and Lübeck.
- IT~emilia given to the Papal States (Legations of Bologna, Ferrara, Ravenna, Forlì, the largest share); the Duchies of Parma and Modena are folded in. Free City of Kraków (1815–46) is inside PL~galicia.
- RS~main: Second Serbian Uprising (1815) won de facto autonomy only by late 1815; shown Ottoman. Ali Pasha of Yanina's semi-independent realm (Albania, Epirus, Thessaly) is shown as Ottoman Rumelia.
- CO: Morillo's reconquest took Cartagena in December 1815, but Bogotá stayed with the patriots until May 1816; shown Spanish (New Granada) per the brief. UY: Artigas's Federal League held the Banda Oriental until the Portuguese invasion of 1816.
- PK given to the Durrani Empire by area: in 1815 Kabul still held or claimed Peshawar, Multan, the Derajat and Kashmir, and the Talpur amirs of Sindh and the Khan of Kalat were (nominal) Durrani tributaries. Ranjit Singh's Sikh Empire held only the central Punjab around Lahore and Amritsar (roughly a tenth of today's Pakistan), so it is not drawn; it takes Multan in 1818, Kashmir in 1819 and Peshawar in 1834.
- IN/BD: East India Company rule mixed with the Maratha states, Rajput states and subsidiary allies (Hyderabad, Mysore); shown as Company India. Nepal is at the end of the Anglo-Nepalese War (Sugauli, Dec 1815/Mar 1816) and keeps its whole modern extent.
- AO, MZ shown Portuguese although real control was coastal and along the Zambezi; ZA shown as Cape Colony although the colony covered roughly its western and southern third. AU shown as New South Wales.
- RU~sakhalin given to Tokugawa Japan (Matsumae posts in southern Sakhalin, Kunashir and Iturup); the northern Kurils were Russian and Sakhalin was largely Ainu and Nivkh land claimed by the Qing.
- KZ shown as indigenous 'Kazakh hordes' (khans still reigned, under nominal Russian suzerainty since the 1730s–40s). TJ given to Bukhara; the mountain begs were largely autonomous. West Uzbekistan (Khorezm) was Khiva's.
- KH: Cambodia was a vassal of both Vietnam and Siam (Vietnamese garrison in Phnom Penh from 1813; Siam held Battambang). LA: Vientiane, Luang Prabang and Champasak were Siamese vassals.
- AZ shown Russian (Gulistan 1813); Nakhchivan and Talysh stayed Persian. AM shown as the Persian Erivan Khanate.
- Polities with "flag": null had no flag the map can show (indigenous, unclaimed, contested, Bhutan before 1949). The app should show a neutral chip for them.
- Polity ids with a year suffix (FR_BOURBON, PT_EMPIRE_1815, OTTOMAN_1815, PERSIA_1815, etc.) are used because the flag differs from the canonical id's later flag; they share the canonical colour code.
- Unclaimed: Spitsbergen, Falklands (abandoned by Spain in 1811), South Georgia (Cook's 1775 claim never followed up), Kerguelen/Crozet (French discoveries, annexed 1893), Bouvet, Heard, Christmas and Cocos Islands, the US Pacific atolls, Antarctica (first sighted in 1820).
- Monaco shown under Sardinia (a Sardinian protectorate from 1815). Switzerland and San Marino are left as themselves.
- Haiti was split between Christophe's Kingdom (north) and Pétion's Republic (south); shown as one Haiti with the Republic's flag.
- Flags with no contemporary national flag use the closest available Commons file: Qing (1862–89 dragon flag), Tokugawa Japan (Hinomaru, used on ships from 1854), Nguyễn Vietnam (army flag captured 1885), Konbaung and Durrani flags are later or reconstructed; Bukhara's and Cambodia's are reconstructions of uncertain date. Kokand (Commons file marked fictional) and Khiva (file shows the 1917–20 flag) are null. Hawaii's flag dates from c.1816 (the Union Jack flew before); the Portugal–Brazil–Algarves flag from 1816; Sweden's union-canton flag from 1815/1818.
- Southern Italy: shown as the Kingdoms of Naples and Sicily under Ferdinand IV/III (restored to Naples May 1815); they were merged as the Two Sicilies only in December 1816. SIAM_1815 and PAPAL_STATES_1815 have their own ids because later eras use SIAM and PAPAL_STATES with different flags.

## 1871: Nations Unite

- ZA is a single unit: assigned to the British Cape Colony and Natal, but the Orange Free State, the South African Republic (Transvaal), Zululand and Griqualand West (annexed by Britain Oct 1871) cannot be drawn.
- DK includes North Schleswig, which was Prussian (German) 1864–1920; no piece exists for it.
- RU~sakhalin made 'contested' (SAKHALIN_SHARED): Sakhalin was a Russo-Japanese condominium (1855–75); the southern Kurils were Japanese and the northern Kurils Russian. Could instead be RU_EMPIRE.
- CN~main includes Xinjiang, which in 1871 was largely Yakub Beg's Kashgaria, and the Ili valley, occupied by Russia in 1871.
- VN is all Đại Nam, though French Cochinchina (southern third, French 1862/67) existed; VN~south (south of 17°N) would wrongly include Hue, so no split was used. The Đại Nam flag is a captured Nguyễn army flag (no clear national flag existed).
- MM is all Konbaung Burma; Lower Burma (Arakan, Tenasserim, Pegu) was British since 1826/1852.
- MY: Straits Settlements (Penang, Malacca) were British; the peninsula's sultanates were independent (Pangkor 1874), the northern ones Siamese tributaries, Sarawak was the Brooke Raj and Sabah under Brunei/Sulu.
- UZ/KG/TJ: Tashkent, Samarkand and northern Kyrgyzstan were Russian; Bukhara (1868) and Kokand were protectorates; Khiva (much of western UZ) stayed independent until 1873.
- PK includes Baluchistan (Khanate of Kalat), outside British India until the 1876 treaty.
- RO~main includes northern Dobruja, Ottoman until 1878; RS~main includes Niš, Pirot and Vranje, Ottoman until 1878. Serbia and Romania are drawn as their own states though still under nominal Ottoman suzerainty until 1878; their flags are their own.
- MD: southern Bessarabia (Cahul, Bolgrad, Izmail) was Moldavian/Romanian 1856–78; whole unit given to Russia.
- HR: Dalmatia and Istria were Austrian crown lands, not part of Croatia-Slavonia.
- CL includes the Atacama littoral (Bolivian) and Tarapacá (Peruvian) until 1879–84; AR and CL Patagonia and Araucanía were still largely indigenous; BR includes Acre (Bolivian until 1903).
- CA includes Newfoundland and Prince Edward Island, separate British colonies in 1871 (PEI joined 1873); the Arctic islands passed to Canada in 1880.
- YE~north assigned Ottoman: the Tihama coast was Ottoman since 1849, but Sana'a and the highlands were taken only in April 1872.
- ER assigned to Ethiopia; Massawa and the coast were Egyptian (transferred 1865). SO~somaliland/SO all indigenous; the Benadir coast was under Zanzibar. TZ/KE coasts were Zanzibar's; interiors independent.
- Africa: SN, GM, SL, AO, MZ, GW assigned to their colonial powers although real control was coastal; GH (Dutch/British coastal forts, Dutch sold theirs to Britain in 1872), GA (French Libreville post), DJ (Obock bought by France 1862, unoccupied) and Lagos (NG) left indigenous.
- FM/PW: Spain claimed the Carolines and Palau but did not occupy them until 1885–86.
- AE (Trucial States) and BH (Bahrain) assigned British on the strength of the 1853 Perpetual Maritime Truce and 1861 treaty; formal exclusive agreements came in 1880–92.
- QA and KW assigned Ottoman following Midhat Pasha's 1871 al-Hasa expedition (Kuwait became an Ottoman kaza, Qatar accepted the Ottoman flag).
- Flags: Russia given the white-blue-red tricolour; the official 'imperial' flag 1858–83 was black-yellow-white (commons:Flag of Russia (1858–1896).svg exists). Ethiopia's pre-1897 flag (Flag of Ethiopia (1270–1897).png) is of uncertain authenticity. Bhutan (BT) left unassigned: it had no national flag in 1871. Tonga (TO) left unassigned: its flag dates from 1866/1875.
- Polity ids with flag-year suffixes (PERSIA_1848, ETHIOPIA_1270, MX_1823, NO_1844, etc.) follow the CA_1868 pattern because the flag differs from later eras; PERSIA and ETHIOPIA_EMPIRE canonical ids were therefore not used.
- TF, AU-HM, AU-CX, SJ, BV treated as unclaimed in 1871 (France formally annexed Kerguelen in 1893; Britain took Christmas Island in 1888 and Heard Island in 1910).
- VA assigned to Italy: after September 1870 the pope kept only the Vatican palaces under the 1871 Law of Guarantees, without recognised sovereignty until 1929.
- ME is all Principality of Montenegro, though in 1871 it was only about a third of today's ME: Nikšić, Podgorica, Bar, Ulcinj (1878–80) and the north (Pljevlja, Bijelo Polje, 1912–13) were Ottoman.
- RS~main also includes Ottoman Sandžak districts (Novi Pazar, Sjenica, Tutin, Prijepolje; Ottoman until 1912). GE includes Adjara/Batumi, Ottoman until 1878.
- JP includes the Ryukyu Kingdom (made a Japanese domain 1872, annexed 1879). KH includes Battambang and Siem Reap, Siamese until 1907.
- YE~south includes Aden, British since 1839 (the hinterland protectorate came in the 1880s). UZ: the Khanate of Khiva (Khorezm, Karakalpakstan) was independent until 1873 and Kokand (Fergana valley) until 1876.
- GT given its Aug 1871 flag (GT_1871, as in later eras); the 1858–1871 flag (commons:Flag of Guatemala (1858–1871).svg) was used until the Liberal Revolution. TT labelled 'Trinidad': Tobago was in the Windward Islands government until 1889.
- Ids aligned with other eras: GREECE_KINGDOM, MUSCAT_OMAN, BHUTAN_1815, GT_1871, HN_1866, HT_1859, PY_1842. Renamed to avoid clashing with other eras' definitions of the same id: PT_EMPIRE_1830 (monarchy flag; PT_EMPIRE elsewhere has the republican flag) and NEJD_1824 (Second Saudi State flag; NEJD in 1914 has the Nejd and Hasa flag). IT_KINGDOM kind set to 'empire' to match its other eras.
- Persia's flag changed (review): the earlier 1834-1906 Commons file carries a {{wrong license}} tag; replaced by Sodacan's CC BY-SA 4.0 'Imperial Standard of Naser al-Din Shah Qajar (1848-1896)', the same white lion-and-sun design for the reigning shah, so the id became PERSIA_1848.
- Events (review): Outer Manchuria (Aigun 1858, Peking 1860) dropped from the cards in favour of Algeria (1830) and Russian Central Asia (1865-68), which change far more of the map since 1815; Alaska folded into the US westward-expansion card. PT_EMPIRE_1815, OTTOMAN_1815, KAZAKH_HORDES, KOKAND and BUKHARA are copied verbatim from 1815.json for the event 'from' flags.

## 1914: The Eve of the Great War

- DK is not cut: North Schleswig (German until 1920) is drawn as Danish.
- TR is not cut: Kars, Ardahan and Batumi area were Russian until 1918; drawn as Ottoman.
- XK split 1913 between Serbia (most) and Montenegro (Metohija west: Peć, Đakovica); whole unit assigned to Serbia.
- GR~north includes Western Thrace (GR-A), which was Bulgarian 1913–1919; drawn as Greek. GR~old includes the Dodecanese (Italian from 1912); drawn as Greek. Northern Epirus disputed with Albania in 1914.
- Kamerun included Neukamerun (1911 strips of today's Gabon, Congo, CAR and Chad) — not drawable; those units stay French Equatorial Africa. CM~british also German.
- PG not cut: whole unit assigned German New Guinea; the southern half was the Australian Territory of Papua.
- SB: Bougainville (in PG today) was German; the rest British Solomon Islands — fine at this granularity.
- TZ not cut: Zanzibar (British protectorate) drawn as German East Africa.
- NA not cut: Walvis Bay (British, part of the Cape) drawn as German South West Africa.
- SO~italian includes Jubaland, which was part of British East Africa until 1925.
- CA not cut: Newfoundland and Labrador was the separate Dominion of Newfoundland in 1914; drawn as Canada.
- CN not cut: leased territories (Kiautschou German, Weihaiwei British, Kwantung Japanese, Kwangchowan French) drawn as ROC.
- IN not cut: French India and Portuguese Goa drawn as British India; princely states not distinguished. Burma was a province of British India, hence the same label.
- MY: Sarawak (Brooke Raj) and North Borneo (chartered company) are included in British Malaya.
- UZ/TJ/TM: the protectorates of Bukhara and Khiva are folded into Turkestan.
- SA~nejd includes the Rashidi Emirate of Jabal Shammar (Ha'il) and the Idrisid Emirate of Asir, both separate in 1914.
- SD: Darfur Sultanate was independent until 1916; drawn as Anglo-Egyptian Sudan.
- KW: Ottoman claim vs British protection (1899); drawn as British. QA: Ottoman garrison remained until 1915; drawn as Ottoman.
- CY/XC: Ottoman sovereignty, British administration since 1878 (annexed Nov 1914); drawn as British. EG: nominally Ottoman, British-occupied; drawn as the Khedivate with its own flag.
- VU New Hebrides was an Anglo-French condominium; assigned to the British Empire.
- AU-HM: British claim (1910) assumed; AU-NF transferred to the Commonwealth of Australia on 1 July 1914, left as Australia.
- TF: Kerguelen (French since 1893); Adélie Land not yet claimed. AQ: British 1908 Falklands Dependencies claim covered part of the peninsula; whole continent drawn unclaimed.
- LI flag 1852–1921 (blue-red, no crown) per Commons file name; Ethiopia flag uses the 1897–1974 file (pennant order may have differed before 1914).
- RU_EMPIRE flag uses the white-blue-red tricolour (national flag from 1896), which equals today's; other eras may need a separate id for the 1858–1896 black-yellow-white flag.
- UNCLAIMED polity has flag null.
- DE~palatinate labelled Prussia though the Palatinate itself was Bavarian; DE~lowersaxony includes Oldenburg, Brunswick and Bremen; DE~saxonyanhalt includes Anhalt.
- VA: the Vatican City state did not exist until 1929; drawn as Italy.
- SJ and BV: terra nullius in 1914.
- HR not cut: Dalmatia and Istria were Austrian crown lands (Cisleithania), Croatia-Slavonia Hungarian; one label covers both.
- TO: Kingdom of Tonga, a British protected state since 1900 (same treatment as 1949).
- BT left as itself: Bhutan had no national flag in 1914 (first flag 1949), so today's flag is anachronistic.
- Balkan Wars event also glows BG~dobruja (Southern Dobruja taken by Romania from Bulgaria in 1913).
- NEJD_HASA (not NEJD): the 1871 era's NEJD is the Second Saudi State with a different flag. QING_1889 (not QING): 1871's QING uses the 1862–1889 triangular flag.
- AT_HU flag is the 1869–1918 civil ensign (the only flag common to both halves), matching 1871; 1923 uses the black-yellow Habsburg flag under the same id.
- BG_KINGDOM: Tsardom of Bulgaria (independent 1908); its white-green-red flag dates from 1879, as today.

## 1923: Empires Fall

- Saar (governed by the League of Nations 1920–35) lies inside DE~palatinate and is shown as German.
- Free City of Danzig (1920–39) lies inside PL~posen and is shown as Polish.
- Memel (LT~klaipeda) is left as Lithuania: seized in January 1923, transfer recognised February 1923, Memel Convention 1924.
- Italian Istria, Zara and the Julian March (and Fiume, a free state until 1924) lie inside HR and SI and are shown as Yugoslav.
- The Dodecanese (Italian 1912–47) lie inside GR~old (GR-L) and are shown as Greek.
- Hatay (Sanjak of Alexandretta, part of French Syria until 1939) lies inside TR and is shown as Turkish.
- Northern Sakhalin was under Japanese occupation 1920–25; shown Soviet (RU~main).
- Finnish Petsamo and Karelia (Vyborg), Estonian Petseri and Latvian Abrene lie inside RU~main and are shown Soviet.
- MD includes Transnistria, which was Soviet Ukrainian (Moldavian ASSR from 1924); all of MD shown as Romanian Bessarabia.
- Crimea was in the Russian SFSR (Crimean ASSR) but is inside UA~main, labelled Ukrainian SSR (same polity).
- Central Asia before the 1924 national delimitation: KZ = Kirghiz ASSR, KG/UZ/TM = Turkestan ASSR, TJ = Bukharan People's Soviet Republic (eastern Bukhara). Western Bukhara (in UZ/TM) and the Khorezm People's Soviet Republic (Khiva) cannot be drawn and are shown as Turkestan ASSR.
- The Rif Republic (1921–26) controlled much of the Spanish zone of Morocco; shown as Spanish Morocco. Cape Juby (Spanish) lies in MA~main and is shown French. Tangier's international status was agreed in December 1923 (in force 1925).
- Zanzibar, a separate British protectorate, lies inside TZ (same polity, label lost).
- The Idrisid Emirate of Asir/Jizan (until 1926–30) lies inside SA~nejd and is shown as Nejd. Interior Oman (Imamate, autonomous after Seeb 1920) shown as Muscat and Oman.
- Newfoundland (a separate dominion, with Labrador) is inside CA and shown as Canada.
- Antarctica: British (Falkland Islands Dependencies 1908/1917) and New Zealand (Ross Dependency, July 1923) claims existed, but AQ is one unit; shown unclaimed.
- Svalbard: the 1920 treaty recognised Norwegian sovereignty, effective only August 1925; Jan Mayen annexed 1929. Shown unclaimed.
- Vatican City did not exist until 1929 (Roman Question); VA shown as Kingdom of Italy.
- Weihaiwei (British until 1930), Kwantung Leased Territory (Japanese) and Guangzhouwan (French) lie inside CN units and are shown as China.
- Iraq (King Faisal, 1921) and Transjordan (Emir Abdullah) had their own rulers and flags but are shown as British mandates per the brief.
- USSR flag: the hammer-and-sickle design was approved in 1923 and formalised in 1924; used Flag_of_the_Soviet_Union_(1924–1936).svg under id USSR_1923 (the 1949 era's USSR uses the 1936 flag).
- South Africa's official flag was the Union Jack; the Red Ensign with the Union shield (same as 1914's ZA_1912) is used as the distinctive flag. The Irish tricolour was used by the Free State though formalised only in 1937.
- Weimar German Länder labels reuse the 1914 groupings (Prussia, Bavaria, ...); Prussia remained a Free State 1918–47.
- Bhutan had no national flag before 1949; assigned BHUTAN_1815 (no flag), as in 1815/1871 (1914 and 1939 leave it implicit, which shows today's flag). El Salvador, Dominican Republic (US occupation 1916–24) and Bulgaria (same tricolour) left implicit. Tonga (British protected state 1900–70) is assigned to the British Empire, as in 1914 and 1939.
- Southern Bessarabia (Budjak, in UA~main) was Romanian 1918–40 but is shown Soviet; Khotyn (in UA~bukovina) is correctly Romanian.
- Jubaland (Oltre Giuba, inside SO) was still part of British Kenya until June 1925; shown Italian.
- Aqaba and Ma'an (inside JO) were administered by the Kingdom of Hejaz until 1925; shown as Transjordan. Al-Jawf (inside SA~hejaz) was taken by Nejd in 1922; shown as Hejaz.
- The Mosul vilayet (northern IQ) was disputed with Turkey until 1926 but administered by British Iraq; shown as Iraq.
- Review (1923): ids renamed to match neighbouring eras: AU_DOMINION, NZ_DOMINION, DK_REALM (1939), TURKEY (1939-2011), EGYPT_KINGDOM (1939/1949), MN_BOGD (1914, same flag; Mongolia kept the Bogd Khanate flag 1921-24), AT_FIRST_REPUBLIC (1939). ROC_1912 is the five-coloured-flag ROC (merged ROC carries the 1928 Blue Sky flag; 1914 should also use ROC_1912). PL_1919 avoids colliding with the implicit modern PL.
- Egypt's 1922 independence was dropped as an event (its colour does not change from 1914's Khedivate, only its flag) in favour of Versailles' European border changes, which are visible on the map.

## 1939: On the Brink of War

- Danzig (Free City, under League of Nations) lies inside PL~posen (PL-22) and is drawn as Polish. PL~posen also holds land that was German in 1939: western Pomorskie (Lauenburg, Bütow, Stolp), the Grenzmark strip of Wielkopolskie (Schneidemühl) and western Upper Silesia in Śląskie (Gleiwitz, Beuthen, Hindenburg). Soldau (Działdowo), Polish, is inside PL~west.
- Sudetenland was annexed directly into Germany (1938) but sits inside CZ, drawn as the Protectorate of Bohemia and Moravia; both are German-held so only the label is approximate.
- Zaolzie (Czech Teschen), annexed by Poland in October 1938, is inside CZ and drawn as part of the Protectorate.
- Polish land east of BY~west (the Vileyka/Hlybokaye area of Wilno voivodeship, now in Minsk and Vitebsk oblasts) is inside BY~main, drawn as Soviet. Interwar Poland and Greater Romania were unitary states: the eastern Polish pieces carry the plain label Poland so no dashed line traces today's PL/UA/BY/LT borders; Romanian labels follow historic provinces as in 1923 (Bukovina spans RO~bukovina and UA~bukovina).
- Southern Slovakia (First Vienna Award, Nov 1938) was Hungarian but is inside SK, drawn as Slovak.
- Dodecanese (Italian since 1912) is inside GR~old (GR-L) and drawn as Greek; Istria, Zara and Fiume (Italian) are inside HR/SI and drawn as Yugoslav.
- Karelian Isthmus, Salla and Petsamo were Finnish in 1939 but lie inside RU~main.
- Transnistria (Moldavian ASSR, Soviet) is inside MD, drawn as Romanian Bessarabia; southern Bessarabia (Budjak) is inside UA~main, drawn as Soviet; Crimea was in the Russian SFSR (1939) but sits in UA~main labelled Ukrainian SSR.
- Newfoundland and Labrador (a British dominion under direct Commission of Government since 1934) is inside CA, drawn as Canada.
- Manchukuo also included Rehe/Jehol (now in Hebei/Inner Mongolia) inside CN~main; Japan's Kwantung Leased Territory is inside CN~manchuria. Japanese-occupied eastern China and its puppet regimes, and French Kwangchowan, are drawn as Republic of China.
- Hatay passed from French Syria to Turkey in June 1939 and is inside TR; correct for summer 1939.
- New Hebrides was an Anglo-French condominium; assigned to GB_EMPIRE with a condominium label. Canton and Enderbury (in KI) became an Anglo-US condominium in 1939.
- Ifni (Spanish) and the Tangier International Zone are not separable; Ifni is inside MA~main (French Morocco), Tangier inside MA~spanish. Cape Juby (the Tarfaya strip, Spanish) is also inside MA~main.
- Zanzibar (British protectorate) is inside TZ, labelled Tanganyika. Sarawak (Brooke Raj) and North Borneo are inside MY, labelled British Malaya and Borneo. Panama Canal Zone (US) is inside PA.
- Bhutan left implicit: a kingdom under British guidance with no national flag until 1949. Antarctica left implicit (British, NZ, French, Australian and Norwegian claims existed by 1939).
- Tuva flag: Commons has 1935-1939 and 1939-1941 versions; the switch date in 1939 is unclear, chose 1935-1939 for summer 1939.
- Iceland (Kingdom of Iceland in personal union with Denmark), Ireland (Éire), Thailand (renamed from Siam on 24 June 1939) and Lithuania/Latvia/Estonia used flags matching today's and are left implicit.
- Polity ids not in the canonical list that other eras may need to reconcile: ES_1938 (Francoist Spain incl. colonies, used instead of ES_EMPIRE because the flag differs), PL_1928, RO_KINGDOM, HU_KINGDOM, GR_KINGDOM, TURKEY, AU_DOMINION, NZ_DOMINION, ZA_1928, CA_1921, US_1912, DK_REALM, MN_1924, IR_PAHLAVI, AF_KINGDOM, SA_1938, IQ_KINGDOM, EGYPT_KINGDOM, YE_KINGDOM, MUSCAT_OMAN, NP_KINGDOM, MX_1934, BR_1889, VE_1930, TIBET, TUVA_1935, SLOVAK_STATE, AT_FIRST_REPUBLIC, AL_KINGDOM, ES_REPUBLIC, HEJAZ, NEJD_1921 (as in 1923).
- USSR uses the 1936-1955 flag; if other eras share the USSR id with a different flag version, they may need splitting.
- Tuva gets TUVA_1935 because 1923's TUVA has a different name and flag. Its 1935 flag formally stayed in use until the 1941 constitution (Wikipedia, Flag of Tuva).
- Bhutan is left implicit, so it would show today's flag, which dates from 1949; Bhutan had no national flag in 1939.

## 1949: A World Divided

- PS is one unit: assigned wholly to Jordan (West Bank); the Gaza Strip was actually under Egyptian administration.
- West Berlin lies inside DE~brandenburg (BB+BE) and is drawn as GDR; the Saar (inside DE~palatinate, RP+SL) was a French protectorate in 1949 but is drawn as FRG.
- Crimea (inside UA~main) belonged to the Russian SFSR until 1954 but is drawn as Ukrainian SSR. Karelo-Finnish SSR is not separable from RU.
- LY: Tripolitania and Cyrenaica were under British and Fezzan under French administration; drawn as GB_EMPIRE.
- SO~italian: still under British military administration at end of 1949; Italian trusteeship began 1 April 1950.
- TZ includes Zanzibar (a separate British protectorate); MY covers Malaya, Sarawak and North Borneo; IN includes Portuguese Goa and French Pondicherry; ID includes Dutch Western New Guinea; JP includes US-administered Ryukyu and Bonin islands; IT~friuli includes the Free Territory of Trieste; PA includes the US Canal Zone; Tangier and Ifni are inside MA.
- Hainan (inside CN~main) was still held by the ROC until April 1950.
- VU (New Hebrides) was an Anglo-French condominium; assigned to GB_EMPIRE with a label saying so.
- AQ left unassigned: overlapping national claims existed (UK, NZ, France, Australia, Norway, Chile, Argentina) but no single polity can be drawn.
- GDR_1949 uses the plain black-red-gold tricolour (emblem added 1959) and colour 'plum' to match GDR in the 1960 fragment (1976 uses 'sage'; reconcile).
- Protected states (Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar, Trucial States, Maldives, Tonga, Brunei) drawn under GB_EMPIRE. Muscat and Oman drawn as its own state.
- Spain uses id ES_1945 (Franco-era flag); Turkey uses TURKEY to match the 2011 fragment.
- Period flags given to Guatemala, Nicaragua, Peru (arms on the flag until 1950), Paraguay (1842–1954), Ecuador (1900–2009), Costa Rica (1906–1964) and Haiti (1859–1964), matching the ids used in 1914. Honduras adopted its five-star flag in Feb 1949, which is today's flag again since 2026, so HN is left unassigned. El Salvador's arms detail ignored.
- Polity ids renamed to match neighbouring eras with the same flag: AF_KINGDOM (1939, 1960), IR_PAHLAVI (1939; 1960 calls it PERSIA), EGYPT_KINGDOM (1939), NP_KINGDOM (1939). Ceylon is CEYLON_1948 because 1960's CEYLON has the 1951 flag. TIBET colour 'amber' to match 1939.
- USSR here carries the 1936–1955 flag, as in 1939; 1960/1976/1990/1993 define USSR with the 1955 flag. The merged file needs one definition or a split id (e.g. USSR_1936).
- Unassigned on purpose: Japan (occupied, same Hinomaru), Italy (republic since 1946, same tricolour), Austria (Allied occupation, still Austria), Thailand (renamed from Siam again in May 1949), Ireland (republic from April 1949), Iceland, Lebanon, Liberia, Latin American states with today's flag. Sikkim (Indian-protected) is inside IN; Saar protectorate inside DE~palatinate.
- Reviewer: Poland now uses PL_1928 (1928–1980 vermilion flag, as in 1939); 1960 uses PL_PRL. Newfoundland joined Canada on 31 March 1949 but CA is a single unit, so no event. ROC colour is TW here but CN in 1939; reconcile. Polity names differ across eras for FRG (1960: West Germany) and PK/JO (1960: Pakistan/Jordan); reconcile on merge.

## 1960: The Year of Africa

## 1976: Decolonisation Nears Its End

- West Berlin was part of neither German state formally but tied to West Germany; it cannot be drawn separately because DE~brandenburg covers Berlin, so all of it is shown as East Germany.
- Sinai (Egypt) and the Golan Heights (Syria) were under Israeli occupation in 1976 but are not separate units, so they stay with Egypt and Syria. The West Bank and Gaza (PS) are shown as a contested area (PS_OCCUPIED, as in 1990/1993) with Israel's flag.
- Western Sahara (EH) is one unit, so the Moroccan north and Mauritanian south (Tiris al-Gharbiyya) are drawn together as contested; the flag shown is the Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic's (proclaimed February 1976), which is debatable.
- East Timor (TL) was annexed by Indonesia in July 1976 but the UN and Portugal never recognised it, so it is drawn as contested (TIMOR_OCCUPIED, the id used in 1993).
- Northern Cyprus (XC) is the Turkish Federated State of Cyprus (1975), unrecognised; it had no distinct flag of its own (Turkey's flag was used), so Turkey's flag is used and it is drawn as contested.
- Namibia is South West Africa under South African rule despite the UN ending the mandate in 1966. Transkei's nominal independence (October 1976) and Walvis Bay cannot be drawn.
- The Panama Canal Zone (US) cannot be split from PA. Kiribati (KI) is labelled Gilbert Islands although Canton and Enderbury were an Anglo-American condominium.
- Sri Lanka's 1972–78 flag differed from today's only in the style of the bo leaves and has no Commons file, so LK is left as itself. Nicaragua, Bhutan, Burundi and similar flags with only minor proportion or shade changes are also left as today.
- Crown dependency Isle of Man is unchanged; Jersey (pre-1981 plain saltire) and Guernsey (pre-1985 St George's cross) are given their older flags as their own polities.
- The Netherlands Antilles (AW, CW, BQ, SX) are given their own polity with the 1959–86 flag rather than NL_EMPIRE, as they were a constituent country of the Kingdom, not a colony; colour follows NL.
- The Northern Mariana Islands (US-MP) began separating from the Trust Territory in 1976; labelled separately but kept within the US polity.

- Polity ids are global across eras, so SD keeps the neutral name 'Sudan'; the 1969–85 name 'Democratic Republic of the Sudan' is the label. Turkey and Ivory Coast use the TURKEY and IVORY_COAST ids from other eras, since the implicit modern names (Türkiye, Côte d'Ivoire) are anachronistic.
